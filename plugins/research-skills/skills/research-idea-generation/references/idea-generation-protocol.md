# Idea Generation Protocol

## Evidence ledger

Separate verified facts, evidence-backed gaps, assumptions, and speculation. A gap is
not a contribution by itself, and absence from retrieved material is not novelty proof.

## Divergence

Generate across mechanism or contribution families: representation, objective,
conditioning, architecture, data/protocol, efficiency, robustness, interpretability,
or application transfer. Require a reason each candidate is not a cosmetic variant.

## Idea contract

Each idea needs a hypothesis, counter-hypothesis, mechanism, falsifier, minimum
validation, kill criterion, resource path, dependencies, failure value, novelty risk,
and feasibility confidence.

## Convergence

Evaluate relevance, distinctiveness, falsifiability, feasibility, evidence need,
information gain, and risk separately. Keep tradeoffs visible. Prefer a portfolio over
several minor variants of one favorite.

## Transfer checks

For application transfer, identify which assumptions survive, which domain mismatch
motivates adaptation, what data or metric changes, and why direct reuse is inadequate.
