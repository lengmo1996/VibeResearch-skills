# Quality Checklist

- Original and normalized identifiers are both retained.
- DOI, PMID, PMCID, and arXiv types remain distinct.
- Every lookup records source, query, timestamp, locator, and response state.
- Metadata strings are treated as untrusted data.
- Every canonical field has provenance and a state.
- Conflicts and legitimate display variants are distinguishable.
- Duplicate candidates are not automatically merged.
- Preprint and published versions remain linked but distinct.
- Zero hit and service failure do not imply nonexistence.
- No external library or manuscript is modified.
