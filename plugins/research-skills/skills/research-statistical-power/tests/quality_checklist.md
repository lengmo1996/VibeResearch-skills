# Quality Checklist

- The `PWR-*` identifier is stable and valid.
- The target quantity is exactly one of sample size, achieved power, or MDE.
- Estimand, analysis unit, test/model, sidedness, and allocation are explicit.
- Alpha and target power are valid for the selected target.
- Effect size has a named scale and a traceable source.
- Unknown assumptions remain unresolved rather than receiving silent defaults.
- Raw and adjusted sample sizes are distinguishable.
- Attrition, clustering, dependence, and multiplicity are handled explicitly.
- Sensitivity analysis covers material uncertainty.
- Calculation method, implementation, version, and rounding are recorded.
- Simulation includes seed, repetitions, model, decision rule, and failure policy.
- Limitations and handoffs are actionable.
- `ready`, `conditional`, and `blocked` verdicts agree with unresolved fields.
