# Publish Venue Targeting Quality Checklist

- [ ] All four Registry modes appear.
- [ ] Paper topic, maturity, contribution, timeline, constraints, and risk are explicit.
- [ ] Current venue facts cite official sources with cycle/date and retrieval date.
- [ ] Historical patterns, inference, and unknowns are not presented as current facts.
- [ ] Hard eligibility constraints precede fit ranking.
- [ ] Fit axes and tradeoffs remain independently visible.
- [ ] Primary, backup, conditional, and not-recommended roles have reasons.
- [ ] Backups have observable switch triggers and decision dates.
- [ ] Revisions distinguish structural mismatch from fixable readiness gaps.
- [ ] No deadline, policy, ranking, rate, metric, review-time, or acceptance outcome is invented.
