# Venue Decision Protocol

## Contribution profile

Record:

- target research community and problem;
- contribution type: theory, method, dataset/benchmark, empirical finding, system,
  application, resource, or combination;
- generality and domain specificity;
- claim/evidence maturity and missing decisive experiment;
- artifact/data/ethics readiness;
- format, timeline, policy, and author constraints;
- risk tolerance and fallback preference.

## Current-fact ledger

| Fact ID | Venue/track | Claim | Official source | Effective cycle/date | Retrieved | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Use venue/track-specific official pages. A prior-year CFP is historical evidence, not
the current rule. When official pages conflict, preserve both and mark the decision
blocked until resolved.

## Eligibility then fit

First apply hard constraints: scope/track, submission type, deadline feasibility,
concurrent-submission, anonymity, page/artifact/data/ethics/AI policies, and any user
constraint. Only eligible or explicitly conditional candidates enter fit ranking.

Then compare independent axes. Do not collapse them into one unexplained score. If a
weighted score is requested, show weights and retain every component.

## Decision roles

- `primary`: best current balance of structural fit, maturity, and timeline.
- `backup`: viable under a named switch condition.
- `conditional`: viable only after a specific evidence/policy constraint clears.
- `not-recommended`: structural mismatch or unjustified effort; state why.

Every switch trigger has an observable event and latest decision date.

## Revision triage

Classify each revision:

- must-have for eligibility or claim support;
- high-value for audience/fit;
- optional polish.

Record effort, dependency, and whether it changes evidence, narrative, artifact, or
only presentation. Venue targeting recommends priorities; it does not invent results.
