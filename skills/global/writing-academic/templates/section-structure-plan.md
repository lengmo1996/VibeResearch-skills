## Consistency checks

- Each paragraph has one dominant function.
- Adjacent paragraphs do not repeat the same job.
- Every comparative or quantitative claim has supplied evidence or a visible gap.
- Terminology, symbols, component order, and claim strength stay consistent.
- The final plan fits the requested section rather than diagnosing the whole paper.
