# Introduction structure guide

Load this guide only for an introduction `outline` or structural `draft`.

## Select an opening

Choose one opening that serves the paper:

- **Problem opening:** lead with a concrete research limitation or operational need.
- **Tension opening:** contrast current capability with a persistent failure mode.
- **Opportunity opening:** identify a newly feasible question, resource, or setting.

Avoid a generic importance claim when the supplied material supports a more specific
entry point.

## Build the story chain

Map the introduction as a sequence:

1. **Scope:** define the problem and why it matters to the intended audience.
2. **Current approach:** summarize what is already possible at the level needed for
   the gap.
3. **Constraint:** state the precise limitation that blocks the desired outcome.
4. **Insight:** explain the supplied observation or design principle that changes the
   problem framing.
5. **Approach:** introduce the work and connect its components to the constraint.
6. **Evidence preview:** state only verified evaluation scope and supported findings.
7. **Contributions:** list distinct, parallel contributions with evidence-bearing
   verbs.

The chain may span several paragraphs, but each paragraph should advance one dominant
function. If two adjacent paragraphs repeat the same function, merge them or sharpen
their roles.

## Contribution paragraph

For each contribution, capture:

- the artifact, method, analysis, dataset, or finding;
- the problem constraint it addresses;
- the evidence that will substantiate it later;
- any scope or uncertainty qualifier that must remain visible.

Do not turn implementation details into contributions unless the verified novelty
depends on them. Do not add “first,” “novel,” or “state of the art” without evidence.

## Draft checks

- The gap follows from the prior context rather than appearing abruptly.
- The approach is introduced before fine-grained module names.
- Contribution bullets are parallel and non-overlapping.
- Previewed results match the experiments section and supplied evidence.
- Related-work detail is deferred unless it is necessary to establish the gap.
